Port Energy ERP - Privacy

Port Energy ERP is the internal business system of Port Energy Private Limited, used only by its authorised staff. It stores company records (purchase, approvals, accounts) in the company's own Google account.

The app may ask permission to send notifications to your phone or computer about approvals, status changes and team chat. A device address used only for this is stored in the company's sheet and removed when the device unsubscribes. Data is not sold or shared with third parties.
